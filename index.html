Tips & Warnings
⚠️ Ses Illetes gate fills up fast. The Ses Salines Natural Park limits vehicle access daily in peak season. If you roll in past 11 AM on a Friday in July, there's a real chance you're turned away or stuck in a long queue. The 10:15 AM target is not optional.
🛵 Don't rent at the dock. The La Savina port on arrival is chaotic — everyone off the boat is competing for vehicles at the same handful of shops. Walk a block off the main drag or (better) book online the day before.
🪪 IDPs are in order. You've already confirmed International Driving Permits for Puglia — the same applies here if anyone is renting a car in Formentera.
☀️ July 17 is a Friday. Day-before-weekend crowds are significant. This makes early arrival at Ses Illetes even more critical, and is exactly why you want lunch and transport locked in rather than winging it.
🌊 Ses Illetes sandbar trick. The beach faces two directions — Ibiza-side is calm and shallow, open-sea-side can be choppy. Pick your side based on conditions that morning.
